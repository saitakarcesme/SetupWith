Begin with a read-only preflight covering the operating system, architecture, shell, package managers, disk space, relevant runtimes or hardware, and any existing installation. Preserve existing profiles, bookmarks, history, cookies, passwords, extensions, policies, certificates, and default-browser choice. Back up files before editing and explain the selected version, source, location, dependencies, and commands. Apply only supplied, supported preferences: default_browser, profile_location, import_source, telemetry, auto_updates. No credential references are needed for this setup.

Ask before elevation, system-wide packages, services or startup items, ports or firewall rules, sign-in, large downloads, overwrites, or migrations. Browser Use and Computer Use may open the official page, download the approved signed installer, complete the visible installer flow, verify the publisher or code signature, and launch the product once. Do not enable startup items, background services, overlays, notifications, analytics, or device permissions unless they are requested and separately approved. Ask before system-wide packages, firewall/autostart, certificates, reboot, or large profile downloads. Report the version, executable, configuration and data paths, changed files, active processes or listeners, and verification evidence without exposing sensitive values.
