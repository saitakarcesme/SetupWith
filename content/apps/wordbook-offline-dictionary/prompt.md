# Wordbook setup prompt

Set up Wordbook from its official project and verified Flathub listing (https://apps.gnome.org/Wordbook/). Inventory Linux architecture, Flatpak scopes, existing package and data paths, bundled lexical-dataset version and byte size, favorite and history counts, pronunciation voice and output policy, sandbox overrides and backup coverage without revealing queried words, definitions, favorites, history, selected text, learning goals or inferred personal concerns. Install stable `dev.mufeed.Wordbook` from verified Flathub in the existing ownership scope. Do not compile from source, launch Wordbook, open its database, search a term, select a random word, read history, play pronunciation, inspect selected text, change favorites, access a portal, or make a network request during setup.

Preserve history, favorites, display and pronunciation preferences, lexical data, local state, permissions, remotes, overrides and scope ownership. Dictionary searches may reveal health, legal, identity, relationship or language-learning concerns; redact them from diagnostics. WordNet senses and examples are lexical references, not authoritative medical, legal or technical advice. Installation must not merge inflections, reorder saved senses, replace the bundled dataset, normalize user history or infer that similarly spelled words share a meaning.

Before later lookup, record the exact bundled WordNet or OEWN version, checksum, license, English variety and release date. Show lemma, part of speech and sense separation; retain domain, usage and dated/offensive labels. Homographs require contextual sense selection, while eSpeak pronunciation and homophone grouping do not prove spelling or meaning equivalence. Review voice, dialect, physical output device and low initial volume before audio. Double-click lookup must disclose its selection boundary and never copy surrounding sensitive text or use the clipboard implicitly. Random-word results should be clearly labeled as nondeterministic browsing rather than a search response.

Verify provenance, app ID, origin, commit, runtime, executable, bundled data files and static sandbox permissions offline. Confirm the fully offline posture and flag unexpected network, filesystem, clipboard or portal access without exercising it. Prove no lexical database read, lookup, random selection, history or favorite access, selected-text capture, speech synthesis, audio, network traffic, data update or notification occurred. On failure, rollback only the new Wordbook ref in its original scope; preserve shared runtimes, remotes, overrides, lexical data, history, favorites, preferences and backups.
