# YouTube setup prompt

Set up YouTube from its verified official source (https://www.youtube.com/). Supported targets: Web. Begin with a read-only preflight covering the operating system, architecture, shell, package managers, disk space, relevant runtimes or hardware, and any existing installation. Open youtube.com over its valid Google certificate and offer the browser's own installed-app capability, without unofficial desktop wrappers, media download tools, codec packs, ad-circumvention extensions, channel automation, or uploader utilities.

Preserve existing Google and YouTube cookies, channel sessions, subscriptions, playlists, watch and search history, caption settings, playback preferences, Premium downloads, creator drafts, upload defaults, and browser notifications. Back up files before editing and explain the selected version, source, location, dependencies, and commands. Apply only supplied, supported preferences: playback_quality, caption_preferences, notification_policy, restricted_mode. No credential references are needed for this setup.

Ask before elevation, system-wide packages, services or startup items, ports or firewall rules, sign-in, large downloads, overwrites, or migrations. Browser Use and Computer Use may open the official page, download the approved signed installer, complete the visible installer flow, verify the publisher or code signature, and launch the product once. Stop and hand control to the user before account creation, sign-in, password entry, MFA, CAPTCHA, age verification, purchases, subscriptions, license acceptance that creates a paid obligation, or linking external accounts. Never bypass DRM, regional restrictions, or platform protections. Do not enable startup items, background services, overlays, notifications, analytics, or device permissions unless they are requested and separately approved. Stop before Google sign-in, MFA, account or channel selection, playback, upload, comment, subscription, notification permission, purchase, Premium enrollment, or download; do not bypass advertisements, DRM, age, region, or creator restrictions. For a large game or media download, report the exact size, destination drive, free space, and bandwidth impact, then wait for approval. Never delete game libraries, save files, media libraries, mods, profiles, or cloud-sync data. If the platform or configuration is unsupported, stop and offer the closest official alternative.

Confirm the installed scope is exactly youtube.com, open only a signed-out landing page without autoplay, inspect browser storage and permissions, and verify no viewing history, upload, comment, subscription, notification, purchase, or download occurred. Report the version, executable, configuration and data paths, changed files, active processes or listeners, and verification evidence without exposing sensitive values. End with manual steps and exact rollback or uninstall instructions that remove only setup-owned changes, restore backups, stop new services, and preserve user data.
